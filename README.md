One sentence, at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life — judged pairwise by an AI judge that rewards surprise. This entry is for the make.fun bounty **Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line** ($2).

## The entry

> The washing machine stopped, and my socks came out of the spin holding on to each other like survivors of the same small storm.

A load of laundry ends, and the most ordinary objects in the house come out looking like they went through something together. The wonder is in the pairing: socks, which everyone expects to lose each other in the wash, instead cling on like survivors.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the sentence above to `/output/entry.txt`. It checks first that the sentence is 1–200 characters and carries no leading or trailing whitespace, so the entry can never fail the gate on length. Run it with Node 22 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```
